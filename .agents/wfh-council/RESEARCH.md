# WaterfallHunter Research Challenger Registry

Every challenger is preregistered before final evaluation. These are hypotheses, not production facts or authorized policy changes.

## H1 — Multi-horizon order flow deterioration

- mechanism: A transition from crowded buying to persistent aggressive sell flow may precede waterfall continuation/reversal failure more reliably than a single taker ratio snapshot.
- point_in_time_requirement: Only trades observable before the candidate decision timestamp; rolling windows must use venue timestamps and causal receipt/freshness checks.
- falsifier: No stable incremental OOS net-R/precision value after existing timing, derivatives and cascade evidence; unstable sign across regimes/symbols.
- promotion_gate: Survives ablation, purged WFO, concentration checks, untouched final OOS and live shadow without increasing stale/unavailable evidence.

## H2 — Futures/spot/mark/index basis stress

- mechanism: Rapid futures-versus-spot or mark/index divergence can expose leverage-driven price discovery and liquidation feedback before/inside cascades.
- point_in_time_requirement: Synchronized same-economic-contract timestamps; never forward-fill across freshness boundaries or mix incompatible index/contract definitions.
- falsifier: Incremental edge disappears after spread/slippage/market-volatility controls or is confined to one venue/event.
- promotion_gate: Stable sign and contribution across held-out symbols/regimes with cost-adjusted benefit and no identity violations.

## H3 — OI × funding × crowding leverage stress

- mechanism: Rising or stressed open interest combined with long crowding/funding and deteriorating taker flow may identify fragile leveraged positioning.
- point_in_time_requirement: Funding/OI/long-short fields must be timestamped and available before the decision; units and sampling intervals must be normalized per venue.
- falsifier: Interaction adds no robust value beyond existing derivatives points, or gains vanish under lagged/stale-safe reconstruction.
- promotion_gate: Positive marginal OOS value, broad regime support and defensible causal provenance.

## H4 — Liquidity / cascade pressure deterioration

- mechanism: A weakening bid stack, widening executable spread/slippage, rising sell-side imbalance and liquidation/cascade pressure may identify when a crowded meme impulse is transitioning into an unstable downside cascade.
- point_in_time_requirement: Order-book, execution, liquidation and trade-flow inputs must be captured causally with venue timestamps, bounded freshness and no post-decision reconstruction.
- falsifier: The interaction adds no stable OOS net-R/precision value after existing execution/order-flow controls, or its apparent edge is explained by stale books and illiquidity artifacts.
- promotion_gate: Stable incremental value across held-out symbols/regimes, acceptable execution capacity, no increase in stale/unavailable evidence and survival under ablation plus cost stress.

## H5 — Relative weakness × market-regime interaction

- mechanism: Meme-specific relative weakness versus BTC/ETH/sector peers may become materially more predictive when conditioned on broad-market direction, volatility and liquidity regime rather than used as a universal static score.
- point_in_time_requirement: Benchmarks, peer baskets and regime labels must be computed only from information available before the candidate decision timestamp with frozen constituent rules.
- falsifier: Sign or value is unstable across regimes, disappears after benchmark beta/volatility controls, or depends on post-hoc peer selection.
- promotion_gate: Positive marginal OOS value with predefined regimes/peer baskets, parameter stability and no concentration in one market episode.

## H6 — Optional attention / reflexivity context

- mechanism: Sudden attention acceleration can amplify reflexive meme positioning and may improve context for crowded-pump fragility when combined with price, derivatives and order-flow evidence.
- point_in_time_requirement: Attention observations must have auditable publication/receipt timestamps and must not backfill future social/news activity into historical decisions.
- falsifier: Attention features add no stable incremental OOS value, merely proxy realized price momentum, or produce venue/source-specific instability.
- promotion_gate: Remains optional context only; promotion requires incremental cost-adjusted OOS value, ablation support and zero conversion of missing attention data into directional bearish evidence.
